Chaukanna News desk

चौकन्ना न्यूज़ (Chaukanna News, @ChaukannaNews) is a Hindi YouTube channel of short news videos on the day's biggest stories in India: cricket, film and TV, politics.

Chaukanna News desk is the private program its owner runs to publish the channel's own Shorts: it uploads each finished Short to the channel and reads the channel's own details and videos. It is not offered to anyone else.
