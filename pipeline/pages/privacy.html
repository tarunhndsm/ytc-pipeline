Privacy policy for Ankahi Kathayein tools

Effective 11 October 2026

Ankahi Kathayein tools is a private program that the owner of the अनकही कथाएँ (Ankahi Kathayein) channel runs for that channel alone. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's permission, it accesses the Ankahi Kathayein YouTube channel through two Google scopes: YouTube (youtube.force-ssl) and YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly). It reads the channel's public details and statistics, the list of its videos, and analytics reports such as views and watch time.

It changes only the channel's own playlists: it creates the channel's series playlists and adds the channel's own Shorts to them. It never uploads, edits, or deletes videos.

How the data is used

Only to measure how the channel's own videos perform, to decide what to make next, and to organize the channel's own videos into playlists.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in token is kept by the owner, on their own computer and as a secret of the owner's automation account. The data read is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone, and not used for advertising.

Ankahi Kathayein tools' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
