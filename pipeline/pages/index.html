अनकही कथाएँ (Ankahi Kathayein, @ankahi.kathayein) tells lesser-known stories from the Ramayana, the Mahabharata and the Puranas in one-minute Hindi videos on Instagram, YouTube and Facebook.

Ankahi Kathayein tools is the private program its owner runs to look after the channel: it reads the YouTube channel's own statistics and files the channel's own Shorts into its playlists. It is not offered to anyone else.
