AI Shortcut desk

AI Shortcut (@AIShortcut-p4k) is an English YouTube channel of short videos on new AI tools, models and research, each checked against its sources.

AI Shortcut desk is the private program its owner runs to publish the channel's own Shorts: it uploads each finished Short to the channel and reads the channel's own details and videos. It is not offered to anyone else.
