Privacy policy for Chaukanna News desk

Effective 20 October 2026

Chaukanna News desk is a private program that the owner of the चौकन्ना न्यूज़ (Chaukanna News) channel runs for that channel alone. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's permission, it accesses the Chaukanna News YouTube channel through two Google scopes: YouTube upload (youtube.upload) and YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly). It uploads the channel's own Shorts, with their titles and descriptions, and reads the channel's details and its list of videos.

It never edits or deletes videos and reads no one else's data.

How the data is used

Only to publish the channel's own videos and to check that they went out.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in token is kept by the owner, on their own computer and as a secret of the owner's automation account. The data read is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone, and not used for advertising.

Chaukanna News desk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

The channel's owner, through the channel's About page.
